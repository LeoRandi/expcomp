# Story planning

Provisional setting supplied by the author on 2026-10-19. This is the current
source of truth for what Cresco's townsfolk may know. No earlier story outline
was found in the project's source or documentation.

## Known to the townsfolk

- Cresco is the only safe place for quite some miles.
- All of humanity now lives in underground tunnels.
- Lately, those tunnels have been filling with monsters from old tales,
  dangerous beasts, and animals.
- Some contact remains with neighbouring towns to the south and east.
- The northern towns are cut off: a rumbling in the tunnels made the previous
  safe routes unavailable.
- Cresco is working to discover another safe northern route.
- The northern road points toward Lubia; its old destination sign is crossed out
  and marked KEEP OUT. The east road points to Fern and the south road to Furion.

## Starting-town implementation

The protagonist starts just outside their home in Cresco. The residents and
their everyday relationships are additions for the playable town, not a wider
plot outline: Mara bakes, Ivo lights the streets, Tamsin
watches the northern approach, Nessa tends herbs, Pell runs deliveries, and Elin
keeps the Resting Lantern inn. Tamsin warns travellers at the north road's
entrance.

Junto is the protagonist's father. His farm lies west of town, enclosed by old,
unattended fences and a shut gate. The protagonist has never tried to tend it.
The entrance sign names JUNTO'S FARM and conveys that personal observation.

A veiled stranger beside the protagonist's home offers transport to the testing
grounds. A Waykeeper there returns the player home. These are development travel
links; their powers, identities, and place in the eventual story are undecided.
The sparring NPC remains in the testing grounds.

## Still open

The reason humanity moved underground, the source of the monsters, the cause of
the rumbling, the protagonist's background, and the main plot have not been
established here. NPC dialogue must not present guesses about them as facts.

Earlier code uses `cresca` in map identifiers and filenames. The authored town
name used in game text is **Cresco**.
