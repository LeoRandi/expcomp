# Provisional story draft

Author-supplied draft, added on 2026-10-19. The text below is preserved
verbatim; see [the story reference](story.md) for the organized outline,
town knowledge, and implementation status.

Long time ago, in this planet, humanoids reign the surface. Valleys, plains, high mountains and deserts, they were all home to different humanoids. Stories have been passed through countless generations of those times, were there was not a ceiling above humans heads, were light was permanent and the air was not cold. Not that this all matters, because one day, everything changed. Some say a god was punished down onto our planet, some say we were the punished ones. Whichever the case, giant columns of a dark, unbreakable material appeared all over the world, and fell from the stars. The pillars, named Needles, pierced the ground, the sacred god of Ga, and destroyed the landscapes we called home. That was not the end though, because the Needles were filled with creatures before unseen, with strange beasts, demons, spirits and, strangelly enough, other humanoids. The monsters raided, killed, destroyed, devoured everything in their way. Kingdoms, countries, entire continents fell in a week, as more creatures poured from inside the needles. The only survivors of this world-wide destruction were the ones that used the Needles, or better said the cracks the needles created, to hide underground. The seemingly infinite caves, caverns and tunnels below the ground became the only safe place. Entrances to the surface were blocked, barricated, or shut off with explosives. The dark ages so begun, with humanity learning to live below the ground. Some towns stablished contact, forming a somewhat safe web of commerce. People still lived, almost thrive below the ground, but the monsters were still leaking on to the planet. Some found their way down the caverns, and some leaked out of holes in Needles deep underground. 

Currently, the town of Cresca is on the far side of the Safe Web, almost the western-most of the web. It has some merchant caravans twice a year, and produces herbs and bread. It has a clean, seemingly infinite well, which is a rare sight. It is also the closest town in the whole underground this close to a Needle, although that is unknown to its inhabitants. but not for long. See, in the north caverns of Cresca, halfway towards Lubia, a small crevice in the wall can led the uncareful traveller to the point of a Needle. This Needle, after some rumblings, has broken part of its side, and small monsters are starting to leak out again. The rumblings have caused the tunnels to collapse at certain points, breaking any connection between the two towns, but a party is forming to discover a new way through. Junto is the only villager to have no stable job in Cresca, meaning it takes any job it can. It then takes responsibility to find a new path towards Lubia, but never returns. His son or daughter, the player, must then follow him onto the north, to find him, and hopefully, a way to reach Lubia.

For this, they will take their father's backpack, which unknown to the player, contains a small ball-like metal creature, a robot companion. The player will venture through the north route, exploring closed tunnel after closed tunnel, until they realize there is only one way forward: a small crevice in the eastern wall of the tunnel. This way, the player will discover their first creature fight, when a bat-like monster tries to bite them to death. The small robot creature will then pop out to save them, and become bonded with the player. After the fight, the robot creature will introduce itself, and follow the player just long enough to find their father, still nowhere to be found. Once the player explores a bit more of the crevice, they will find the entrance to the Needle, and a clue which shall hint that Junto had to hide inside the Needle, and will have to quickly retreat to Cresca. There, in the Inn, they will explain what happened to the guard and inn-keeper. Both will have an argument wether or not to send someone else to the north to find the path to Lubia, but it matters little to the player, who will venture back up again after getting some preparation. 

The game loop of this part of the game will consist on the player going back and forth between Cresca and the Needle, which has leaked out enough creatures to make the tunnel a dangerous place. The player will learn to fight, and even bond with some of the creatures. Some of them will drop meat or gems when defeated. Some creatures will ask the player to adopt them in exchange for a piece of meat, or gems, or different, yet unknown items. 

The player will also be able to venture into other towns, like Fern or Furion, to the east and south, whose paths have also become strangely infected with creatures coming from the needle. Once the player has become strong enough, they can then venture into the Needle, head first, to find their dad. The Needle will contain a one-floor dungeon, full of traps, new monsters, some loot, and finally, a pseudo-dragon boss. The player, upon defeating this formidable foe, will realize the floor has "healed", lit up, and monsters now appear in way lesser amount. Their creatures, current and future, will also get their level cap to level 10 instead of 5. Each boss or strong creature must give the player a level cap increase of 5, meaning their creatures may gain up to 5 levels more when gaining experience.
